## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
AgriConnect is a digital marketplace designed to connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers without depending on multiple intermediaries.

Farmers can register on the platform and upload details about their products, quantities, quality, and prices. Consumers and bulk buyers can browse available products and place orders directly from farmers.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to forecast product demand, suggest suitable prices, and identify efficient delivery routes. It also provides logistics support to ensure that agricultural products reach buyers on time.

## Technical Approach
Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
Backend: Python with Flask.
Database: MySQL.
Artificial Intelligence: Machine learning for demand forecasting.
Maps and Logistics: Maps API for route optimization.
Platform: Web-based digital marketplace.
## Feasibility and Viability
The proposed solution can be developed using existing web technologies and AI tools. It can initially be implemented in selected villages and expanded to other regions.

Possible challenges include farmers' lack of digital awareness, transportation costs, and product quality management. These can be addressed through a simple user interface, local support centres, and verified product listings.

## Impact and Benefits
Farmers: Better earnings by reducing middlemen.
Consumers: Fresh agricultural products at affordable prices.
Bulk Buyers: Direct access to farmers and larger quantities.
Environment: Reduced food wastage and efficient transportation.
Rural Development: Improved digital access to agricultural markets.
